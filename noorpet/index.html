The path

Everything you need to read your first verses

A narrow, honest learning loop — letters first, then words, then verses. Built to feel like a quiet daily ritual rather than a test.

All 32 letters, four forms

Names, sounds and transliteration, with isolated, initial, medial and final forms shown side by side — plus a full letter chart that is always one tap away.

Harakat and joining

Fatha, kasra, damma and sukun; sun and moon letters; the six non-connectors. See exactly how letters flow together before you meet them in a verse.

360 Quranic & MSA words

Curated vocabulary with English and Bangla meanings. Switch between a Quranic-first path, everyday Modern Standard Arabic, or both together.

Quran verses, word by word

A small curated set of passages with per-word breakdown, transliteration and a gentle reflection prompt. Quran text is sourced, not paraphrased.

Dhikr counter with sources

Sixteen well-known adhkar with Arabic, transliteration, meaning and a reference for each — from the Quran and authentic hadith collections.

Glows offline, speaks Arabic

Everything works without a network. Audio uses the device Arabic (ar-SA) voice, so no download is required. Gentle word reminders every 10 minutes are optional.

Principles

Built to be clean, quiet and beneficial

NoorPet is a study tool, so the content and the experience were kept simple on purpose. There is no background music, no depiction of living beings, no advertising and no analytics. The companion is Noor — a lantern, not an animal — and every Quranic and dhikr text carries its source.

وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

“My Lord, increase me in knowledge.” — Qur’an 20:114

These are product design choices, not a religious ruling. If you follow a particular school of thought, use your own judgement and, where needed, the advice of a qualified scholar. Sound can be disabled entirely in Settings.

  • No music, no songsOnly short, soft, non-melodic chimes for answers and reminders — and you can switch them off.
  • No depictions of living beingsThe mascot is a lantern. The launcher icon is a crescent and light, not a face or animal.
  • Sourced Quran and dhikr textUthmani Quran text (Tanzil), Pickthall English, Muhiuddin Khan Bangla; adhkar referenced to Qur’an and authentic hadith.
  • No ads, no tracking, no accountProgress stays on your device. No microphone, no server, nothing sent anywhere.
  • Respectful by defaultArabic content is right-to-left, the UI stays in English or Bangla, and nothing interrupts your reading with pop-ups.

The project

A small companion, not a gamified app

NoorPet began with a simple idea: what if learning to read Arabic felt like tending a small light? Instead of a streak counter that shouts, there is Noor — a lantern that brightens when you practise and dims when you rest.

The loop is deliberately short. Learn, Review and Progress keep you moving between seeing a letter, hearing it, answering honestly, and returning to the ones you missed. Quests and milestones exist, but they never block the content.

It is built for phones first, runs entirely without a server, and is modelled on the KanaPet Japanese-kana trainer. The Android app wraps the same web code in Capacitor, so there is one codebase and no hidden services.
