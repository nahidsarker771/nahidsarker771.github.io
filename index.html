the lore

how the sausage gets made.

No agency, no team standup, no “circling back”. Just a student, a laptop, and a suspicious number of unfinished tabs.

🎓

student first

Classes, assignments and exams come first. Apps get built in the gaps — which is exactly why they stay small and focused.

🌙

vibe coder, not buzzword coder

I open the editor, follow the idea, and ship when it feels right. Then I fix the bugs my future self leaves in the comments.

🪫

allergic to bloat

No login screen for a to-do list. No 40 MB SDK for a button. If a feature doesn’t earn its place, it doesn’t ship.

✈️

offline > cloud

Every app here works in airplane mode. Your data stays on your device, and “server down” is not a support ticket I have to answer.

🚀

ship it, then polish

A working v1 beats a perfect v0. I’d rather have four real apps on GitHub than one imaginary startup in a Notion doc.

🫶

free means free

No ads, no subscriptions, no “pro” pop-up. If you like something, star the repo — that’s the whole business model.

open source

steal my code. it's public.

Fork it, break it, learn from it. Every project is on GitHub with the real commits — including the questionable ones.
